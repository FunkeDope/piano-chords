Colours

    

    
Each chord is coloured by its root note: a rainbow from C, sharps sit in between. A shaded card or chord is a 7th or extended chord.

  


  

    
Reading the diagrams

    
    
      
  • Darker key: left hand
    • 
      
  • Lighter key: right hand
    • 
      
  • 1–5Fingers: 1 is the thumb, 5 the pinky
    • 
      
  • ⇄Inversion: the lowest note isn't the chord's name note. Hover or tap the circle for why
    •

Start / stop auto-scroll (the practice ball when practising)

    More tips
    
    
      
  • The key is set from the tab's capo and tuning, so it matches the recording.
    • 
      
  • Fret-number tab shows the sounding notes underneath.
    • 
      
  • Chord cards show the shape you play most often in the song. Normal chords keeps every chord in root position; Real play uses inversions so your hand barely moves.
    • 
      
  • Practice keyboard: arrows show which finger slides to which key next; a gold ring means that finger stays put.
    • 
      
  • Drag the practice panel by its top bar to float it anywhere; double-click the bar to dock it back.
    • 
      
  • On phones, tapping a chord pops up its diagram. Drag it anywhere and it stays there; tap it to close, double-tap its top bar to send it back.
    • 
      
  • Songs are saved in this browser. Use Export / Import in Songs to move them to another browser.
